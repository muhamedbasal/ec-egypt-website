FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What pulses and grains do you supply?
A range of world pulses and grains — chickpeas, whole and split lentils, Australian, English and Lithuanian faba beans, lupine and basmati rice — for wholesale and food manufacture.
Do you supply wholesalers and food manufacturers?
Yes. We supply wholesalers and food manufacturers in Egypt and surrounding countries, with personal attention given to every shipment.
How do I request a quote or a sample?
Tap “Inquire” on any product to message us on WhatsApp, or use the contact page. Tell us the product, grade and quantity and we'll reply quickly.
Which regions do you serve?
We serve customers in Egypt and surrounding countries, and we handle the export documentation needed to move product to the end user.
In which languages can I contact you?
Our team works in both English and Arabic. You can reach us by WhatsApp, phone or email.

Need a quote?

Tell us your product, grade and quantity — we'll get back to you quickly.
