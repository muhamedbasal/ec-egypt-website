Buying chickpeas: whole servings or smooth hummus?

Choose your finished-product test first. Then compare size specifications and the usable chickpea output from each sample.

Home / Products / Chickpeas buying guide

Which chickpeas should you request?

For a whole-chickpea dish, test how evenly the grains cook and how well they keep the appearance you need. For hummus, test softness and the texture after your normal blending process. A size label alone does not tell you which sample will give the best result or lowest raw-material cost per usable kilogram.

EC’s catalogue lists Chickpeas 10 mm, Chickpeas 14 mm and Chickpeas. These are catalogue labels. Ask for the measurement method, actual size distribution and a representative sample of the offered lot before treating a label as an agreed specification. Confirm origin and current availability with EC.

Use two different acceptance tests

USA Pulses’ chickpea guide (PDF) includes whole and pureed uses and notes that longer-stored dry chickpeas can take longer to soften. That supports testing the actual sample; it does not establish a cooking time or yield for EC’s products.

Ask what the size and quality description means

  • Size basis: is the description based on a sieve or another measurement? What size range and proportion will the supplier agree for the lot? Do not assume every grain measures exactly the catalogue number.
  • Separate quality factors: ask how colour, damaged grains, mechanical damage or splits, and foreign material are described and measured. The Canadian Grain Commission’s chickpea guide treats these separately. Its grading system is a reference, not a claim of Canadian origin, certification or grade for EC stock.
  • Lot identity: record the sample reference and ask whether it represents the lot being offered. Attach the accepted sample and agreed specification to the buying discussion; repeat the application test if the offered lot changes.

Compare usable output before comparing the quote

Use equal weighed dry inputs and record the same preparation, equipment, water source, cooking endpoint and drainage method for each trial. Follow your facility’s validated process. Do not change preparation and raw material together when trying to explain a different result.

Copy one record per chickpea sample
FieldWhat your trial record needs
Dry inputSample/lot reference, application and dry chickpeas weighed into the trial, in kg. Record sorting loss separately.
Preparation and cookingPreparation conditions, water used, equipment, process time and the same intended softness endpoint.
Drained outputTotal cooked chickpea weight after a defined, repeatable drainage step. Record the drainage time and method.
Accepted outputWeight of chickpeas meeting your application criteria after removing rejects and, if your process uses them, skins or coarse sieve residue. Weigh before adding tahini, oil, water or other recipe ingredients.
DecisionAccepted output ÷ dry input, both in kg; texture/intact-grain result; accept or retest, with reason.

Usable-output ratio = accepted cooked chickpea kg ÷ dry input kg. For arithmetic only, a fictional trial with 1 kg dry input and 2.4 kg accepted drained chickpeas gives 2.4 kg/kg. This is not an EC yield claim or a target. Extra retained water is not evidence of better quality; keep the drainage and acceptance rules comparable.

Raw-material cost per accepted cooked kg = quoted price per dry kg ÷ usable-output ratio. Use a positive measured ratio and compare quotes on the same currency, delivery and tax basis. This calculation excludes energy, labour, water, other ingredients and packing, so it is not a total production cost. Do not substitute finished hummus weight for accepted chickpea weight.

Request the chickpea sample and quote you need

Include the catalogue product, intended whole-serving or puree use, quantity and unit, delivery city/country, pack requirement, sample reference and the size/quality points you want confirmed. Ask EC about sample arrangements, available lot, minimum order, price validity and delivery terms. No price, stock, pack size or delivery date is set by this guide.
