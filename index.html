About us

Built on family experience, focused on reliable supply.

Egyptian Canadian Company represents the third generation of the Basal family in the agricultural commodities trade, built on a family heritage dating back to 1947. We specialize in trading, processing and packing pulses, grains and food commodities for the Egyptian and Arab markets.
